What this site is

DiviStay is a learning project that demonstrates group hotel booking with split payments. It was designed by Noya, Amit and Amir as part of a Software Engineering course, and built by Noya Adivi.

No real bookings or payments

  • The hotels, rooms and prices are examples only.
  • Payments go through a simulated provider. Nothing is ever charged, and you should not enter a real card.
  • Messages are shown on screen and are not sent by email.
